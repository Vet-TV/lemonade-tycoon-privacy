This Privacy Policy describes how Vet GamingTV ("we", "us", "our") handles information for Lemonade Tycoon - WebUI Edition (the "Game") distributed via the Microsoft Store. This policy is required under Microsoft Store Policy 10.5.1.

Summary in plain English: The Game does not collect, transmit, or sell your personal information. It runs locally on your device. Your lemonade stand progress is saved locally. We do not use analytics, advertising SDKs, or third-party trackers inside the Game.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information. Specifically:

  • No name, email, address, or contact details
  • No location data
  • No photos, contacts, or files
  • No advertising ID or cross-app tracking
  • No voice, video, or biometric data

The only data processed is gameplay data (e.g., money, inventory, day number) stored locally using your browser/app local storage. This data never leaves your device unless you manually export it.

2. Information Collected by Microsoft Store

If you download the Game from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft may collect information as part of the Store experience (e.g., purchase history, device info) under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. We do not receive or control that data.

3. How We Use Information

Since we collect no personal information, there is no use for marketing, profiling, or selling. Local gameplay data is used solely to save and restore your game progress.

4. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not share, sell, rent, or transmit any personal information to third parties. There are no third-party SDKs in the Game.

5. Data Storage and Security

All game progress is stored locally on your device via WebView2 / localStorage. Uninstalling the Game or clearing app data will delete this local data. We take reasonable steps to ensure the Game does not transmit data externally.

6. Children's Privacy

The Game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your Rights and Choices

Because no personal data is collected, there is no data to access, correct, or delete on our servers. You can at any time delete local save data via the in-game reset or by clearing the app's storage in Windows Settings > Apps.

8. Third-Party Links

The Game does not contain external links. This privacy policy page itself is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may collect standard server logs (IP address) per GitHub's Privacy Statement.
